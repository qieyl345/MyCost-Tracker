# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback about work, not personal attacks.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information, credentials, or private data.
- Keep contributions and discussions focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through [GitHub](https://github.com/qieyl345). Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. The maintainer will review reports and determine an appropriate response; no response time is guaranteed.
